# Finance portal

## 1. Purpose and assumptions

This spec owns the screens Northwind's Finance staff (`finance` role) use to move money through the product: settle or fail the payments clients start (deposits included), record a deposit paid outside the product, find and void invoices, see what clients owe, and run billing. Invoice and payment rules (pay calendar, statuses, payment lifecycle, the billing run, deposits) live in [billing-and-invoicing.md](./billing-and-invoicing.md); this document says how they are shown and which operations each button calls. The client's view of the same data is [client-finance.md](./client-finance.md).

Assumptions:

- Every read goes through `src/server/finance-lists.ts` (the Payments, Invoices and Receivables lists and the drawer's records, over `src/server/billing-queries.ts`), `src/server/billing-queries.ts` (receivables) or `src/server/finance-portal-queries.ts` (home tiles, company options, tab counts, the PDF input) and every write through `src/server/billing.ts`. The screens contain no billing arithmetic.
- Everything here is for `finance` only. Each query and operation asserts the role itself (`FORBIDDEN_ROLE`), the pages sit under the `/finance` portal guard (acl F2), and the Server Actions call `requireRole("finance")` first (acl F1, §6).
- Money is whole US cents shown through `formatCents`; days are `YYYY-MM-DD` and print through `formatIsoDay`. "Settled" is how the UI says a payment whose stored status is `succeeded`.
- The Finance Home (`/finance`, §2.6) is this slice's: a figures line (to settle, overdue, ready to bill), billed vs collected across the page, then "To do", the first payments to settle with their Settle, all filterable to one client. Finance has no task list: its only task, `confirm_deposit`, is a deposit payment, so it waits at the top of To settle and of "Waiting on you", and every Finance task link (`taskPageHref`) goes to `/finance/payments`.

There is no Deposits page and no Finance task page: `/finance/deposits` and `/finance/tasks/[taskId]` are gone (404). A deposit the client has paid is a payment to settle (§2.2); an unpaid one is an invoice (Kind: Deposit) with **Mark as paid**.

Nav (in order): Home, Payments (count of payments to settle, deposits included: `paymentStatusCounts(...).initiated`), Invoices, Receivables, Billing run. Each list item is active on its own path and below it (`prefix`).

Page states: every data page is a synchronous export wrapping an async child in `Suspense` with a skeleton that mirrors the layout (L2a); empty results use `EmptyState` (C7); a throw lands on `src/app/finance/error.tsx` (an `Alert` with Try again). Pending list navigation dims the rows and shows a pulse bar.

### 2.0 Lists

Payments is a queue, so it is a C43 dense list (`DenseRow`): rows with a bottom border, no column headers, one main action per row, sections and bulk selection. Invoices is a set of records looked up and compared across columns, so it is a C6 table (ui-guidelines C30). In both the whole row opens the staff drawer in place (`RowOpener`, `useListCursor`: `j`/`k` and Enter; a modified click or a new tab follows the row's `?open=` link). No ids are printed; a payment's id is the drawer's "Reference" fact. Search is debounced 300 ms into the URL; the selects write the URL at once; Clear (shown only while a filter is on) resets search and filters. The shared pagination bar (C11) shows above ten rows, sizes 10, 25, 50 and 100. Every list query runs on the server (`financePaymentsPage`, `financeInvoicesPage`), which also returns the ids of the whole filtered list for the drawer's Previous and Next. Receivables' table is the Invoices table with Days late for Status (§2.5), paged the same way by `financeReceivablesPage`; a row opens the Invoices drawer rather than a drawer of its own.

**Payments.** A row: a checkbox (on To settle; it shows on hover or while anything is selected), the client with a muted second line, the day and wait, the amount, then **Settle** and a "More actions" menu with **Mark failed** on To settle rows.

- The second line (`paymentRowDetail`): one invoice is "INV-2026-0055 · Dev Petrova"; several are "6 invoices · October payroll" (one pay month) or "6 invoices"; a deposit adds "Deposit · starts Nov 2"; a failed payment shows its reason instead.
- To settle: deposits first (an order is waiting on them), then the longest-waiting first. The wait reads "Oct 21 · 3 days" (America/New_York business days, `settleWait`) and turns danger once it is more than three business days old.
- Settled: grouped by the month the payment settled, newest first (`DenseSection`, collapsible), with the settled day. Failed: flat, newest failure first, with the reason as the second line and the failed day.
- Selecting rows shows a sticky bar: "N payments selected", Clear and **Settle N · $41,200** (whole dollars; the dialog states the cents). After a bulk settle the bar says what happened ("Settled 3 payments · $41,200.18", and "1 payment not settled: <why>") until Done.
- Empty states per tab ("Nothing to settle…", "No settled payments yet…", "No failed payments…"); a filtered empty list says "No payments match these filters." with Clear.

**Invoices.** `PageHeader` "Invoices" with no description, then one control row (search, Status, Client, More; it does not wrap from 1024px), the chips, the summary caption and the table (`InvoicesTable`, `src/components/finance-portal/invoices-table.tsx`).

- Columns, one line each: **Invoice** (the number), **Person** (a muted dash when none), **Client** (muted, truncated with a `title`), **Due** ("Oct 30"; the year only when it is not the current business year in America/New_York, `formatIsoDayInYear` with the page's `today`; muted once paid or void), **Status** (dot and word, state only: Unpaid, Overdue, Processing, Paid, Void), **Amount** (exact, right-aligned). No sortable headers. Kind, period, issued, due and paid days are in the drawer (§2.5), not the row.
- Order: one fixed order, newest issue date first and then the higher number (`compareNewestInvoice`, applied in `financeInvoicesPage`); the drawer's Previous and Next follow it. There is no sort control or `sort` parameter.
- Status words: Finance reads an `open` invoice "Unpaid" (`STAFF_INVOICE_STATUS_LABEL`), in the table, the Status filter and its chip, and the staff drawer, because the Due column already says "Due". The filter's URL value stays `due` (Unpaid or Overdue), so any `?client=<id>&status=due` (and an old `?company=<id>&status=due`) keeps working; the stored status and the client portal's "Due" are unchanged.
- Void invoices are left out unless the Status filter is Void.
- The summary caption covers the whole filtered list in exact money: "279 invoices · $2,031,442.10 total · $258,899.33 outstanding" (`invoicesSummaryText`; outstanding is Unpaid plus Overdue). It is left out when nothing matches; the empty state says so.
- Chips: one per filter on (Status, Client, Kind, Issued range; the search is not a chip), each with a remove button. The More button counts its filters on ("More (2)"). Kind stays a filter though rows no longer show it.
- Narrow widths: Client folds away first, then Person, by the table's own width (a container query, so the sidebar counts); the page never scrolls sideways.
- States: `InvoicesSkeleton` (the control row, the caption, the header band and ten rows); pending navigation dims the table under a pulse bar; "No invoices yet. Run billing to issue the first ones." with nothing at all; "No invoices match these filters." with Clear when filtered.

### 2.1 Settling and failing a payment

`settlePayment` marks the payment `succeeded`, its invoices read as paid and, when an invoice is a deposit whose case has a ready `confirm_deposit` task, completes that task with resolution `settled` in the same transaction, so onboarding moves on. Both settling and failing are refused (`ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`) for a payment that is no longer initiated; the UI says "That payment is no longer waiting on Finance: it was already settled or marked failed. Refresh the page."

- **Settle** (row and drawer): dialog "Settle $6,782.84 from Acme Robotics?", confirm "Settle $6,782.84", pending "Settling…". The description says the invoices become Paid and, for a deposit, that it confirms the person's deposit.
- **Settle N · $X** (bulk bar): dialog "Settle 3 payments?" listing each with its amount, confirm "Settle $41,200.18". It runs `settlePayment` once per payment, each in its own transaction, so one stale payment does not hold the rest back; the bar reports what settled and what did not. There is no keyboard shortcut for settling.
- **Mark failed** (drawer, or the row's menu, which opens the drawer with the form showing): an inline form in the drawer body, "Why did it fail? *" (required, 300 at most), one line of consequence ("INV-2026-0055 and 2 other invoices become payable again." and, for a deposit whose order still waits on it, ", and the client's Pay deposit step reopens.", then "The client sees the reason."), Cancel and a danger **Mark failed** ("Marking failed…"). A refusal shows in the form ("Nothing was changed") and changes nothing.

`failPaymentAction` picks the server function per case: a payment covering a deposit whose open order has a ready `confirm_deposit` goes through `markDepositNotReceived(caseId, reason, paymentId)` (the reason is stored as "Deposit not received: <reason>" and the Pay deposit step reopens, §2.2); every other payment goes through `failPayment`. The pinned `paymentId` makes a stale second press fail with the payment wording instead of failing a newer payment of the same order.

### 2.2 Deposits in Payments and Invoices

- A deposit the client has paid is an initiated payment: it is in Payments > To settle, listed first, with "Deposit · starts Nov 2" in its second line and, in the drawer, "Deposit for Dev Petrova, who starts Nov 7. Settling it confirms the deposit, so onboarding can move on." (or "The order was cancelled." for a cancelled order).
- **Settle** completes `confirm_deposit` (§2.1).
- **Mark failed** on a deposit fails the payment through `markDepositNotReceived`: the deposit invoice derives back to Due (or Overdue past its date) and appears again in the client's Invoices tab with Pay, and the client's Pay the deposit step reopens with a note (billing-and-invoicing B8, tasks-and-workflow §5.8). A payment that covers other invoices fails as a whole; the consequence line says how many others become payable again. A cancelled case's deposit payment has no step to reopen: it fails through `failPayment`, which voids the unpaid deposit, and the consequence line says so ("INV-2026-0039 is voided, since the order was cancelled.").
- An unpaid deposit with nothing in flight is found in Invoices (Kind: Deposit). While its order is open and a deposit step (`pay_deposit` or `confirm_deposit`) is ready, its drawer offers **Mark as paid** (a text button): the confirm dialog "Mark INV-2026-0040 as paid?" says "Use this when the client paid outside the product.", pending "Marking paid…". It calls `markDepositReceived(caseId)`, which records a settled payment without a saved method and completes the deposit steps.
- The engine's `pay_deposit` and `confirm_deposit` tasks are unchanged; only where Finance acts on them moved.

### 2.3 Voiding an invoice

`voidInvoice(invoiceId, reason)` cancels an invoice nothing has paid or started to pay, and withdraws its pay slip. In the drawer it is the "More actions" menu's **Void invoice**, shown only while the invoice is Unpaid or Overdue: a destructive dialog "Void INV-2026-0281?", a required reason (kept on the invoice), Keep invoice and **Void invoice** ("Voiding…"). An invoice with a payment (processing or paid) or one already void is refused; the dialog says so ("That invoice has a payment (processing or paid), so it cannot be voided. Fail the payment first." or "That invoice is already void."). Void invoices are left out of the default invoice list; filter by status Void to see them.

### 2.5 The staff drawer

One drawer per screen (`StaffDrawer`, `src/components/finance-portal/staff-drawer.tsx`), the client's own panels (`PaymentPanel`, `InvoicePanel`) with Finance's slots (ui-guidelines C45, staff variant):

- Payment: title "Payment of $X" with its status, the client as subtitle, Settle and Mark failed while it is to settle, the stepper. Facts: Client, Method, Started, Settled or Failed on with the Reason, Reference (the id). The invoices it covers; each opens the invoice in place with Back.
- Invoice: number and status (an open invoice reads "Unpaid", §2.0), kind subtitle ("Deposit invoice"); actions **Mark as paid** (unpaid deposit only, §2.2), the Download PDF icon, the "More actions" menu (Void invoice, §2.3). Facts: Client, For, Service period, Pay date (payroll), Issued, Due, Paid (once paid), Void reason. The covered invoices of a payment use the same "Unpaid" word. "Payments on this invoice": each opens the payment in place with Back. A compact History (issued, each payment started, settled or failed, voided with the reason).
- Previous and Next walk the filtered list the drawer was opened from (the whole list, not only the page). After an action the drawer stays on the record and reloads it with the page.
- `?open=` deep links: the page loads the linked record whatever the filters (a payment also picks its tab unless the link names one); closing the drawer drops `open` from the URL and keeps the tab.

### 2.4 Billing run

`previewBillingRun` is read-only. It plans with the same pure function the run uses (`planInvoices` in `billing-core.ts`), so what the preview lists is what Run billing issues. It returns what would be issued today (counts of payroll and deposit invoices, pay slips, totals, by client, the invoices), the next three pay periods with an estimate for the people billable today, and the most recent issue dates with what was issued on each (runs are not logged, so history is invoices grouped by issue date).

**Run billing** calls `runBilling` for today and reports invoices issued (payroll and deposit), pay slips created and the total; it is idempotent, so pressing it again says "Nothing to issue. Everything due as of <day> is already billed." The machine trigger `POST /api/billing/run` (see the route table in [architecture.md](./architecture.md)) is unchanged.

The page (`src/app/finance/billing/page.tsx`, components in `src/components/finance-portal/billing/`, wording in `src/lib/billing-run-view.ts`) is two columns from `lg` (3/5 and 2/5) that stack below it:

- **Run card** (`BillingRunCard`): "Due to issue" as of today and the page's only primary button, "Run billing · N invoices" ("Running…" while it runs). Its body is the three figures Payroll invoices, Deposit invoices and Total (each with its count) and the pay slips the run creates. With nothing due the body is "Nothing to issue. Everything due as of Oct 24, 2026 is already billed." and the button is disabled. After a run the outcome replaces the figures (`role="status"`): "Issued 14 invoices totalling $212,300.00" over "12 payroll invoices and 2 deposit invoices · 12 pay slips created", or the nothing-to-issue sentence when a page that was open before another run presses it. A failure keeps the figures and shows a destructive alert with "Try again". There is no confirm dialog: the figures above the button are the confirmation, and a second run issues nothing.
- **By client** (`BillingByClient`): the same invoices, one row per client (invoice count and total, largest first) that expands to its invoices (person, kind, amount); "Nothing waiting to be issued." when empty.
- **Next pay periods**: the `UpcomingBillingChart` of `billingCadence` (§4.1), issued periods then estimates.
- **Recent issue dates** (`RecentIssueDates`): the last five issue dates with the invoices issued and their total.

### 2.5 Receivables

Who owes Northwind, how late, and which invoices to chase first. The page is read-only; settling, voiding and reminders happen on Payments and Invoices. The page (`src/app/finance/receivables/page.tsx`) reads `clientAgeing` (§4.1) once per request and `financeReceivablesPage` (§4) for the table; components in `src/components/finance-portal/receivables/` and `charts/bar-list.tsx`, URL state, wording and view models in `src/lib/receivables-view.ts`. Layout rules are ui-guidelines C32 and CH9–CH10.

- **Header**: "Receivables", no description, and on the right the **Client filter** (`ClientFilter` in `finance-portal/client-filter.tsx`, shared with the Home): "All clients" or one client that owes money, written as `?client=<companyId>` with the other parameters kept and `page` dropped. It narrows the figures, By age and the table; it is absent while the book owes nothing.
- **Figures line** (`ReceivablesFigures`): one quiet `text-sm` line, "$258,899 outstanding · $217,021 overdue · $95,566 in flight". Whole dollars on screen, the exact amount in the `title` and in screen-reader text. Overdue is in the danger tone while it is above zero. In flight is muted and links to `/finance/payments`: it is money in a payment that has not settled, so it is never an invoice of the table. Outstanding and overdue follow the filters; in flight shows only with no filter on (it is a figure of the whole book, not of a bucket or a client).
- **By age** (`BarList variant="section"`, full width, between the figures and the table): the heading "By age" (`text-base font-semibold`, as the Home's chart) with the takeaway muted beside it ("83% overdue of $259K outstanding", `ageTakeaway`) and a hairline under it. Current, 1–30, 31–60 and 60+ days late, each with its invoice count, its amount and a bar scaled to the longest row; tones neutral, caution, alert, critical (status tokens, never the brand colour). A $0 row is a disabled button with a muted amount and no bar. Every row filters: it is a link with `role="button"` and `aria-pressed` (Enter and Space; cmd-click opens the filtered view in a new tab), named like "1 to 30 days late: $217,021.45, 36 invoices, selected". The selected row is outlined with a check and a bolder label; the other rows keep full contrast. An sr-only table of the rows, and an empty state when nothing is drawn. By age is drawn from the book filtered by the selected client only, so its bars never move when one of its rows is clicked; the figures and the table follow both filters.
- **URL state**: `?bucket=current|1-30|31-60|60-plus` and `?client=<companyId>`, combined (AND). A By age row's link toggles `bucket`, and the Client filter sets `client`; each keeps every other parameter (the page size included) and drops `page`. Values are parsed defensively: an unknown bucket (an old `in-flight` included), a malformed id, or a client that owes nothing is ignored. A plain click navigates softly in a transition; the figures and the table dim with a refetch bar until the server answers.
- **Unpaid invoices**: the heading `text-base font-semibold` with the count after a muted dot ("Unpaid invoices · 40", as the Home's "To do · 5"), and beside it the chip of the selected age ("1–30 days late") with a ghost **Clear** that removes only the age and keeps the client (the Invoices pattern, `FilterChips` in `list-controls.tsx`). The client has no chip: the header filter shows it.
- **Table**: the unpaid invoices (Unpaid or Overdue) the filters select, from `financeReceivablesPage`, in one fixed order: the oldest due date first, ties by invoice number (`compareChaseOrder`). It is the Invoices table (`InvoicesTable variant="receivables"`): Invoice (the client muted beneath the number), Person, Due, **Days late** ("17 days late" in the danger tone, "due today", "in 6 days" muted) and Amount, with the same container-query hiding of Person. A row opens `/finance/invoices?open=<invoiceId>` (`financeInvoiceHref`); `j`/`k` and Enter work. Paged on the server with the shared `UrlPagination`. A selection with no invoices reads "No unpaid invoices match these filters." with a Clear.
- **Agreement**: the table's summary, the figures line and the By age rows are the same numbers for every bucket and client (guarded in `finance-lists.test.ts` on the seeded database and end to end).
- **Nothing owed**: when the whole book owes nothing, the page is one muted line, "Nothing is owed.", with the money in flight beside it when there is some; no charts and no table.
- **Loading and errors**: `ReceivablesSkeleton` mirrors the layout (the figures line, the By age section of four rows, the table's heading and header band over rows); errors use the portal's error boundary.
- **Removed** with this design: the headline card, the ranked client list, the client drawer (`?open=<companyId>`, `loadClientReceivableAction`, `src/app/finance/receivables/actions.ts`, `src/server/client-receivables.ts`, `src/lib/finance-links.ts`), the description, the takeaway and the footer. `AgeingBar` and `clientAgeing` stay (the Home uses them).

### 2.6 Home

What needs Finance today, and how the book is doing. The page (`src/app/finance/page.tsx`) is server rendered; components in `src/components/finance-portal/home/`, wording in `src/lib/finance-home-view.ts`; layout rules are ui-guidelines C33.

- **Header**: "Finance", with the Eastern business day under it ("Saturday, Oct 24", `homeDateLabel(businessDayOf(now))`), and on the right the **Client filter** (`ClientFilter`, shared with Receivables): "All clients" or one client, written as `?client=<companyId>` (`parseHomeClient`, `homeHref`; an id that is not a client is ignored). It narrows the figures line, the chart and To do to that client; the page has nothing else to filter by.
- **Figures line** (`HomeFigures`): one quiet line of three links in whole dollars (exact in `title` and for screen readers), no boxes, no icons.
  - **To settle** → Payments: "$95,566 to settle" (the total of the initiated payments; the count is the nav's Payments count and the To settle tab's, and the To do header's), or "Nothing to settle". Its link, and Overdue's, carry the client filter (`/finance/payments?client=<id>`, `/finance/receivables?client=<id>`).
  - **Overdue** → Receivables: "$217,021 overdue" in the danger tone, or "Nothing overdue". The figure is the Receivables page's overdue headline (`clientAgeing(...).bookAgeing`, or one client's row, `ageingTotalsFor`).
  - **Ready to bill** → Billing run: "$16,603 ready to bill" while invoices are due (what Run billing would issue; the run itself is confirmed on Billing run by its figures), else "Next billing Fri Oct 30" (the next issue date of `previewBillingRun(...).upcoming`) or "No billing scheduled".
- **Billed vs collected** (full width, above To do; `BilledVsCollectedChart` `variant="section"` over the last 8 periods, narrowed to the client's invoices): one bar per pay period as tall as what was billed, the part collected in the brand colour and the part not yet collected in its tint; the takeaway "79% collected of $1.1M billed", the legend, the tooltip and the table carry the rest.
- **To do** (`FinanceTodo`; named as the client Home's list is): the header "To do · 5" with the count of every payment to settle and a ghost "View all" (Payments, narrowed to the client), then the first five payments in To settle's order (deposits first, then the longest-waiting), as a table like Invoices with the columns Invoice, Due, Amount and an unlabelled actions column: the invoice number it settles ("INV-2026-0055", or "6 invoices") with the client muted beneath, the day it is due to be settled (three business days after it started; "Oct 26", danger once past), the amount and an outline **Settle**. A deposit is not marked on its row; it is simply first. The row is a stretched link to `/finance/payments?open=<paymentId>`. Settle opens the same confirm as Payments (`SettleDialog`, §2.1); once it succeeds the page refreshes, the row leaves, the figure and nav count drop by one, and a quiet `role="status"` line beside the heading says "Settled $18,599.94 from Acme Robotics." (no toast). With nothing to settle the list is one muted line, "You're all caught up."
- **Loading**: the header, filter, figures line, chart and list each stream in their own `Suspense`, with skeletons of the same shape (`HomeFiguresSkeleton`, `HomeChartsSkeleton`, `WaitingSkeleton`). The "Who owes us" section is gone: the ageing and the per-client split are Receivables. Only `HomeSettleButton` (with its dialog) and the status line are client components.

Data, per request (`cache`d, so one session, clock and database): `financeOverview(db, user, now, { book, companyId })` for the figures line, which plans the billing run once (`previewBillingRun`); `clientAgeing` once, read once for the figures line; `billedVsCollectedByPeriod(db, user, now, 8, companyId)`; `oldestWaitingPayments(db, user, now, 5, companyId)`; `listCompanyOptions` for the filter. `companyId` is the validated `?client=` (or `null`). The Home replaced the Finance task inbox (`TaskInboxHome`, `TaskInboxTable` and Finance's `TASK_INBOX_CONFIG` entry), the four `FinanceOverviewTiles` (with their "Deposits awaiting" tile) and `listPendingDeposits`.

## 3. Server Actions (`src/app/finance/actions.ts`)

All return `ActionResult` (acl §6): `requireRole("finance")` before anything else, identity from the session only, ids narrowed to non-empty strings of at most 120 characters, reasons trimmed and capped at 300. A success revalidates `/finance` and `/client` (layouts), so counts, nav badges and the client's invoice list refresh.

| Action | Calls | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `settlePaymentAction(paymentId)` | `settlePayment` | |
| `settlePaymentsAction(paymentIds)` | `settlePayment` per id | 1 to 100 distinct ids; returns `{ settled, failed[] }` |
| `failPaymentAction(paymentId, reason)` | `markDepositNotReceived` for a deposit whose order waits on it, else `failPayment` | reason required (§2.1) |
| `markDepositReceivedAction(caseId)` | `markDepositReceived` | Mark as paid (§2.2) |
| `voidInvoiceAction(invoiceId, reason)` | `voidInvoice` | reason required |
| `runBillingAction()` | `runBilling` | returns `{ today, invoices, payrollInvoices, depositInvoices, payslips, totalCents }` |
| `loadFinancePaymentAction(paymentId)`, `loadFinanceInvoiceAction(invoiceId)`, `loadFinanceInvoiceDetailAction(invoiceId)` | `financePaymentRecord`, `financeInvoiceRecord`, `financeInvoiceDetail` | the drawer's records off the page; throw on a refused role, `null` for a bad or unknown id |

A refused role is `forbidden`, no session `unauthenticated`, a bad argument `validation` (with the field in `details`), a domain refusal its `DomainErrorCode`. `ILLEGAL_TRANSITION` is turned into a sentence by what was attempted (`illegalTransitionMessage` in `src/server/action-result.ts`): the payment, invoice and deposit wordings above and below, and the order lifecycle ones ("This person has already started, so the order can no longer be cancelled. End their employment instead."); an unrecognised one reads "That is not possible right now. Refresh the page and try again." Users never see the code. The deposit one: "This order has no deposit step waiting for Finance. Refresh the page."

### 4.1 Insight read models (charts)

`src/server/finance-insights.ts` feeds the Finance charts (ui-guidelines §5.8). Each function takes the database, the actor and `now`, asserts `finance` (`FORBIDDEN_ROLE` otherwise) and returns plain data whose shapes the chart props accept as they are; the arithmetic lives in exported pure functions tested on their own. Days are business days in America/New_York (`businessDayOf`), periods come from the pay calendar, and void invoices and failed payments count nowhere.

| Function | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `billedVsCollectedByPeriod(db, actor, now, periods = 8, companyId = null)` | `BilledVsCollected`: the last `periods` pay periods whose issue date is on or before today, oldest first; per period its dates, `billedCents` and `invoiceCount` (payroll invoices of that service period), `collectedCents` and `paidInvoiceCount` (settled allocations to them), `inFlightCents` and `inFlightInvoiceCount` (initiated allocations); `totals` over the window; and `deposits` (deposit invoices issued after the issue date of the period before the window, with the same three figures), which belong to no pay period |
| `oldestWaitingPayments(db, actor, now, limit = 5, companyId = null)` | `OldestWaitingPayments` (the Home's "To do"): initiated payments in To settle's order, those covering a deposit first, then oldest `initiatedAt` first (then id), each with the client, amount, `initiatedDay`, `businessDaysWaited` (weekdays after that day up to today, `businessDaysBetween`), the invoice count, the first invoice (lowest number, with the person) and the deposit it covers, if any (soonest start); and `total` (count and cents of every waiting payment, not only the listed ones) |
| `clientAgeing(db, actor, now)` | `ClientAgeing`: built on `receivables` (`billing-queries.ts`) and the Receivables page's data (§2.5). `clients` is one `ClientAgeingRow` per client with money outstanding or in flight (buckets, in flight, outstanding, overdue cents and count, `oldestDueDate`, `oldestOverdueDays`), most overdue first; `bookAgeing` is the same `AgeingTotals` for the whole book. Outstanding excludes money in flight, as on the Receivables page |
| `billingCadence(db, actor, now, upcoming?)` | `BillingCadence`: the last 3 pay periods with payroll invoices issued on or before today (non-void, by service period, oldest first; `kind: "issued"`), then the next 3 from `previewBillingRun(...).upcoming` (`kind: "estimate"`; pass `upcoming` when the page already has the preview, so the run is planned once). A period due today that has not been run yet is in neither half |

The pure parts are `billedPeriodWindow`, `summarizeBilledVsCollected`, `waitingPaymentFrom`, `ageingFromReceivables` and `cadenceFrom`. The charts' own helpers (takeaway sentences, axis and segment labels) are in `src/lib/finance-chart-view.ts`.

The Home's wording is pure in `src/lib/finance-home-view.ts`: `homeDateLabel`, `homeFigures` (each figure's amount, words, link and danger flag), `parseHomeClient`, `homeHref`, `paymentsHref` and `waitingTitle` (a row's invoice number, or "6 invoices"). The wait itself is `waitAge` in `src/lib/finance-portal-view.ts` (the day it started, how long, the day it is due to be settled and whether that has passed), which `settleWait` on Payments also uses.
